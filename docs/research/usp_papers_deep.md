## S1. Refined USP (stress-tested; 5 lines for the slide)
1. **Recovered a Hikvision recorder's footage from under a DIFFERENT vendor's reformat, and measured it against the drive's own surviving index (99.6%).** No paper or rival found tests a cross-vendor reformat (searched again today: none). SWGDE 17-V-002 names reformat-on-reinsertion as a risk. Say "measured against the disk's own partial index", never "beats 91.8%".
2. **Time you can defend: the recorder's own log matched a video gap to the minute, and on NIST's public DVR image we show the stored times are local display time labelled UTC with the clock 8 h off; another SIH team publishes that timeline as UTC.** (Trace docs/real-corpus-validation.md.) No paper corroborates log events with video gaps.
3. **Validation earned on known datasets, as SWGDE 18-Q-001 §5.3.2 and UK FSR-G-218 §7.4 require: native export, the disk's own index, the recorder's own per-frame container. Nothing is called validated before a byte-match.** OpenDHFS, BhanuPrasad and vidrensic share the "no upgrading" idea; none has real media.
4. **Real media from three recorder families (Dahua/CP Plus DHFS, Hikvision, HeimVision), failures published.** No paper or rival found reports real-media results on more than two families. Rzayeva 2025 has two (lab drives); Yoon & Hwang one.
5. **Cameras split, never guessed, where every frame says channel 0: counter-evidence on real NVR drives to the 2026 DHAV demultiplexing paper's assumption.** The principle is shared (Park & Lee 2014; OpenDHFS "no unsupported attribution"; CARVE groups by OCR/PRNU). The field evidence is ours.

## 8. Validation standards - exact hooks (read full text; saved in papers/) (01:48-01:51)
- SWGDE 18-Q-001 v2.1 (7 Mar 2024) "Minimum Requirements for Testing Tools Used in Digital and Multimedia Forensics" [read full text] https://www.swgde.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/2024-03-07-SWGDE-Minimum-Requirements-for-Testing-Tools-Used-in-Digital-and-Multimedia-Forensics-18-Q-001-2.1.pdf . §5.3.2 Recovery tools: must be tested with a KNOWN DATASET (carvers: >=1 contiguous + >=1 fragmented file in unallocated space; FS-based recovery: contiguous, fragmented and partially overwritten deleted files); warns tools "can combine fragments from different files" so recovered data must be checked in operational context; test before service and after major revision. §5.3.3 timeline/log tools: known dataset, comparison, or manual verification of a subset. DVRs are listed among media for write-blocker testing.
  => Mapping: the team's known datasets are (a) the recorder's own native export (D1 byte-match), (b) the surviving on-disk index (D3 coverage), (c) the HeimVision per-frame container (D13 carver score), (d) generated twin cameras (D4). SWGDE's "combine fragments from different files" risk = camera mixing, which D4/D13 measure. Almost no rival and no paper frames its evaluation in SWGDE 18-Q-001 terms.

This argues for a tool that detects and labels UNKNOWN formats honestly (detected_not_parsed) and adds vendors by plugin, as the HeimVision route showed. Encryption at rest may appear, which is gap G3.

## 20. ISO wording (02:02) [secondary summaries; standards themselves are paywalled - not bought]
- ISO/IEC 17025:2017 cl. 7.2.2.1: the lab "shall validate non-standard methods, laboratory-developed methods and standard methods used outside their intended scope or otherwise modified". The listed validation techniques include reference standards/materials, a systematic assessment of the factors that influence the result, robustness under controlled variation, comparison with results of other validated methods, and interlaboratory comparison (https://iso-cert.uk/iso-17025-method-validation-and-verification/).
  => Mapping:
    - reference material: the public NIST CFReDS image;
    - comparison with another validated method: byte-match against the recorder's own export;
    - factors that influence the result: the status is set by the weakest evidence, and limits are stated;
    - interlaboratory comparison: G5 (run the free rival tools on the same images).
  A DVR parser is by definition a "laboratory-developed method", so any lab using it must validate it. The tool ships that record.
- ISO/IEC 27042:2015: analysis and interpretation must address "continuity, validity, reproducibility and repeatability", record enough to allow "independent scrutiny", and justify the choice of method (https://www.iso.org/standard/44406.html abstract).
- ISO/IEC 27041:2015: tool VERIFICATION against declared requirements reduces the method VALIDATION needed (section 6).
- Safe wording for judges: "aligned with" / "produces the records required by", NEVER "ISO-certified" or "compliant". Certification applies to a lab, not a tool. A rival (TraceX) claims an "ISO/IEC 27037-compliant dossier", which is a claim a judge can knock down.

## 21. WFS paper - now read in full (02:03) [read full text; papers/batista_brito_2022_wfs.pdf; OA https://www.atenaeditora.com.br/catalogo/dowload-post/65625]
- Galileu Batista de Sousa (IFRN; Policia Federal, Natal) & Unaldo de Oliveira Brito (IFRN), "Analysis and recovery of videos from the WFS file system", Sci. J. Applied Social and Clinical Science 2(8), 2022, doi:10.22533/at.ed.216282205059 (CC BY-NC-ND). The first author is the "Batista" of gbatmobile/dhfs_extractor (the Brazilian Federal Police practitioner behind the public DHFS extractor that rival BhanuPrasad cites).
- WFS (low-cost DVRs): superblock, index area, data area and fragment descriptors dissected; 4-byte packed timestamp (6 bits year, 4 month, 5 day, 5 hour, 6 minute, 6 second) where "file systems used in DVR" generally use 6 bytes; built a Python WFS extractor plus a WFS recognizer inside The Sleuth Kit 4.9.0 (so TSK tools browse WFS videos); demonstrated on "a real example of media". States that TSK, FTK, EnCase and IPED do not support these FS. On the commercial tools HX-Recovery for DVR and DVR Examiner: "these programs have errors in recovering videos"; they helped early on but became "obstacles" (qualitative; no numbers; free versions only).
- Further practitioner refs it cites (Brazil/Russia): Dresch, Gouveia & Zago 2016 (WFS timestamps, casework); Tomilin 2016 (WFS internals); Yancharsky 2018 (WFS) [cited by others].
- Could NOT: measure recovery against ground truth; report on Hik/Dahua; handle time zones.
- => Useful citation for "commercial tools are black boxes with known errors" (a named law-enforcement author), and for the design choice of plugging into standard toolchains (TSK) rather than a closed GUI.
